# Reference photographs

Photographs of real trees to set the species against: each tree whole,
open-grown and seen side-on, and its foliage close to. They are on Wikimedia
Commons under free licences; this repository links to them and holds none of
them. Open a photograph beside the lab's *One tree* (the species, *Standing*)
and compare: the outline, how high the crown starts, how open it is, how the
leaves gather, the colours in daylight.
